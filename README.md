## How tutoring works

<p align="center">
  <img src="docs/assets/learning-loop.jpg" alt="The Lex tutoring loop: encounter, attempt, focused feedback, repair, variation, and retrieval" width="100%">
</p>

Lex treats real communication as the curriculum:

- **Useful first.** Begin with language you can send, say, understand, or rehearse now.
- **Attempt before lecture.** Your first try becomes evidence of what already works and what remains fragile.
- **Focused correction.** Lex prioritizes the choice that most affects meaning, relationship, naturalness, or transfer.
- **Repair and reuse.** Use the correction again under a changed cue instead of merely reading about it.
- **Inspectable continuity.** Optional learner-owned state records what was new, supported, independent, or transferred—without pretending to issue an official proficiency score.

## Translation that fits the situation

<p align="center">
  <img src="docs/assets/translation-fit.jpg" alt="Translation fit accounts for audience, purpose, relationship, locale, register, protected terms, and ambiguity" width="100%">
</p>

Lex preserves what a text is **doing**, not only what its words denote. Nontrivial translation can account for purpose, audience, relationship, target variety, register, protected terminology, placeholders, formatting, and source ambiguity. When consequential human authority matters, Lex prepares the work for qualified review rather than impersonating certification.

## Try it

> I need to tell my new neighbor in Mexican Spanish that their music is carrying into my apartment, but I want to stay friendly. Help me say it, explain the one choice that most affects the tone, then rehearse their likely reply with me.

A strong response gives you usable language immediately, makes the relationship choice visible, and lets you practice the repair—not a generic lecture about Spanish politeness.

## Trust boundary

Lex does **not** issue official ACTFL, CEFR, IELTS, ILR, or other proficiency ratings. It does not hear audio that was never supplied, turn cultural tendencies into universal rules, or certify consequential translations.

For consequential work, Lex can clarify the brief, draft or compare language, preserve terminology, expose ambiguity, and prepare a reviewer handoff. Qualified human authority remains qualified human authority.
