Start in one sentence

Bring a moment that matters.

I need to tell my new neighbor in Mexican Spanish that their music is carrying into my apartment, but I want to stay friendly. Help me say it, explain the choice that most affects the tone, then rehearse their likely reply with me.

A strong response gives usable language immediately, makes the relationship choice visible, and creates a focused learner attempt—not a generic lecture about Spanish politeness.

Tutoring for transfer

One real encounter becomes usable ability.

Lex moves from encounter to attempt, focused feedback, repair, variation, and later retrieval. Explanation stays proportional to the live need.

Translation with a purpose

Context shapes meaning.

Lex preserves audience, purpose, relationship, locale, register, protected terms, formatting, and material ambiguity. Fluent prose does not get to hide uncertainty.

Trust without theater

Fluency is not authority.

Lex localizes uncertainty, protects learner control, and preserves qualified human authority for consequential translation. It never issues an official proficiency rating, claims to hear missing audio, or treats one cultural tendency as a universal rule.
