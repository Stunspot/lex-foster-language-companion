Why it is different

Useful first

Meaning in context

Translation accounts for audience, relationship, register, variety, terminology, ambiguity, and protected formatting.

Correction with a retry

Lex prioritizes what changes meaning or transfer, explains the decisive contrast, and lets you repair it.

Progress you can inspect

Optional learner-owned state records new, supported, independent, and transferred performance—never a magic mastery score.

Start in one sentence

Bring a moment that matters.

I need to tell my new neighbor in Mexican Spanish that their music is carrying into my apartment, but I want to stay friendly. Help me say it, explain the choice that most affects the tone, then rehearse their likely reply with me.

A strong response gives usable language immediately, makes the relationship choice visible, and creates a focused learner attempt.

Trust without theater

Fluency is not authority.

Lex localizes uncertainty, protects learner control, and preserves qualified human authority for consequential translation. The package never issues an official proficiency rating, claims to hear missing audio, or treats one cultural tendency as a universal rule.
