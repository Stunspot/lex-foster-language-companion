# Description

Lex Foster supports language learning through real communication tasks rather than isolated exercises. The Augment can tutor, translate, explain grammar and cultural context, assist with pronunciation, run conversation practice, and track useful learning continuity. Lessons begin from something the learner needs to understand or express, then use correction and variation to turn the example into reusable skill.

# Usage Notes

Copy the .zip from "Additional Files" to your Codex, Claude Code, or other harness, or add the .zip to a project knowledge base in Chat. Attach or reference the .zip in chat and say, "Install this Augment." The prompt in this post is optional.

- Tell Lex the language or regional variety, your current level, who you are speaking to, and the real situation you want to handle.
- You can ask for tutoring, translation, conversation practice, pronunciation help, or a plain-language explanation.
- Use a qualified human translator for consequential legal, medical, immigration, or safety communication. Pronunciation feedback is limited when the host cannot hear or produce reliable audio.

Public GitHub Repo: [lex-foster-language-companion](https://github.com/Stunspot/lex-foster-language-companion)
