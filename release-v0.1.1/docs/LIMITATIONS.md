# Limits and non-claims

Read this page before treating fluent output as authoritative.

## Language coverage varies

The active model may handle many languages, but quality is not equal across languages, dialects, registers, domains, scripts, or communities. A confident tone does not measure competence. Lex should localize uncertainty to the affected form and ask for community or qualified review when consequence warrants it.

## Text is not audio

Without supplied audio, Lex cannot hear pronunciation, fluency, timing, tone, or listening comprehension. Text-based pronunciation support is preparation, not an observed assessment.

## Informal evidence is not certification

A chat sample can support a bounded description of what the learner did in that task. It cannot produce an official proficiency credential or infer unexamined speaking, listening, reading, or writing ability.

## Translation is purpose-bound

A translation may be useful for conversation, learning, drafting, or internal understanding while remaining unfit for filing, care, safety, contract, publication, or other consequential reliance. Obtain qualified review when the domain, jurisdiction, language pair, community, or certification requirement demands it.

## Culture is variable

Cultural and protocol guidance describes patterns in a named context. It is not a universal rule about a nation, ethnicity, religion, generation, profession, or language community. Personal preference and local authority can supersede a general tendency.

## Learner state is optional and partial

A learner profile records selected evidence and preferences. It may be stale, incomplete, or contradicted by current performance. The learner owns corrections and deletion.

## Host behavior remains host-specific

Static package validation does not establish installation, discovery, activation, resource loading, Python execution, persistence, restart behavior, browser accessibility, or future host compatibility. Check the [Host matrix](../HOST-MATRIX.md).

## No outcome guarantee

The package cannot guarantee fluency, retention, travel success, relationship repair, workplace outcome, exam result, translation acceptance, or avoidance of misunderstanding. It provides a stronger process and clearer evidence boundaries; the world remains irritatingly allowed to contain other people.
