# Public provenance

Lex Foster Language Companion is a Collaborative Dynamics public-service Augment.

## Identity and source custody

The public practitioner is a grown derivative of Sam Walker's canonical **Universal Language Tutor — Lex Foster T1 v1.2**. Sam clarified during this build that Lex is a big Black Nigerian man in his thirties. The public persona and documentation preserve that identity.

A privately retained protocol-droid persona informed translation layers, meaning preservation, etiquette, and protocol analysis. Its third-party character identity, likeness, and source text are excluded from the repository and release.
